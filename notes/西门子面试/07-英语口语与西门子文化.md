## 二、项目英文介绍（用 STAR，30 秒/项）

**AI-Notebook**
> It's a searchable knowledge platform for agent system design — 24 research tracks, around 190 long-form articles. I built it with React, TypeScript and Vite, with a custom content pipeline that generates structured data and search metadata. The interesting part was performance: articles load on demand and the search index is only fetched when you open search, so the first screen stays fast. I also built automated browser tests across every article route, because a beautiful homepage with broken deep links is still broken.

**Agent automation system**
> I built a system where AI agents take features from a backlog, implement them in isolated git worktrees, and must pass automated verification before anything is merged. If verification fails, the change is rolled back automatically. The hard problem wasn't generating code — it was defining "verified" precisely enough that the agents can't claim success without evidence.

## 三、常见英文追问 + 简答模板

**Q: What was the biggest challenge in your projects?**
> Parallelism and trust. Multiple agents working on the same repository conflict with each other, so I isolated every task in its own worktree and serialized the deployment path. And I learned that agents will confidently report "done" when they're not — so every task has to prove itself with an executable check.

**Q: Why Siemens Healthineers?**
> Two reasons. First, the domain: healthcare software has a much higher bar for correctness and traceability, and that's exactly where my strengths are — verification, rollback, auditability. Second, the scale of impact: the systems here affect real clinical workflows, not just engagement metrics.

**Q: What do you know about our culture?**
> From what I've read and heard, Siemens Healthineers values long-term engineering quality and clinical responsibility over short-term speed — "we don't ship until it's safe" is closer to the culture than "move fast and break things". I also understand the company operates with a strong emphasis on quality management systems and regulatory compliance, which shapes how engineering decisions are made.

**Q: Why are you leaving your current role?**
> I'm looking for more complex, higher-stakes problems. My current work is broad, but the domain constraints are relatively light. I want to work where correctness and reliability are non-negotiable, and I believe that environment will make me a better engineer.

**Q: Where do you see yourself in five years?**
> Technically, I'd like to grow into the person who owns system-level design decisions — the one others come to for architecture trade-offs. I'm also interested in the reliability side: building the practices that keep complex systems trustworthy. I'd rather go deep in one domain than stay shallow across many.

## 四、英语表达的三个提分细节

1. **主动用结构词**：`First / Second / The key trade-off is / To be concrete` —— 让面试官听出逻辑。
2. **不会的词就换说法**，不要卡住沉默：`I don't remember the exact term, but what I mean is...`
3. **语速慢一点，句子短一点**：非母语者犯的最大错误是长句 + 快语速，导致听不清。
